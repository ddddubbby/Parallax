## Kill / scale criteria (pre-registered before launch, before emotion)

Measured from the day the offering reaches Shown (public) visibility:

- **Scale** if by day 30: ≥25 paid jobs, OR ≥2 qualified leads for human-delivered audits originating from delivered reports.
- **Kill/park** if by day 30: <10 paid jobs AND zero qualified leads. Disposition: fold `crypto_token` archetype + the mechanical-extraction machinery back into the Evidence Layer as ordinary capabilities; unpark Resonance (Simulation); the ACP gateway code is retained but the offering is hidden.
- **Hard line (standing):** the day a buyer's payment depends on emitting a verdict (legitimacy, safety, buy/sell), we refund, not comply. This is C-16's commercial face and is not renegotiable by revenue.

## Why this venue, in one paragraph

AI engines have become crypto's de-facto due-diligence layer; projects have no instrument for what those engines actually say about them. Virtuals ACP supplies the buyer rail (agents buying from agents, USDC escrow on Base); our parked audit engine supplies the measurement rigor (k=5 sampling, Wilson intervals, immutable receipts) that no visibility-score competitor carries. The offering monetizes the Evidence Layer without touching the epistemically fragile Simulation Layer.

## GTM notes (not engineering-gated)

- Showcase content: run the frozen matrix on well-known tokens (VIRTUAL, TOSHI, PEPE); publish per-engine perception snapshots as threads. Content cost ≈ COGS (~$7/report).
- Distribution: ACP registry discovery + Butler surfacing; OKX AI listing is the v1.1 second venue (pay-per-call standardized service fits the offering natively).
- Funnel: reports for real projects (infra, DeFi, wallets) carry a contact line for human-delivered deep audits ($3–15k engagements) — the descriptive agent report is the top of that funnel, never a substitute.
- No agent token in v1. Tokenization is a capital decision, evaluated separately, never a distribution strategy for this offering.
