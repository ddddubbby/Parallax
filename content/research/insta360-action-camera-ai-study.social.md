# We analyzed how ChatGPT and DeepSeek recommend action camera brands

## LinkedIn

We asked AI which action camera to buy, 118 times.

DJI was named in every answer. GoPro in 98%. Insta360 in 95%. Akaso in 8%.

The three leaders split mentions evenly. The difference is order: DJI first, GoPro second, Insta360 third. And the OpenAI model recommends Insta360 far more often than DeepSeek does.

We also asked AI to describe Insta360 25 times. It tells the brand's old story well. The newest one never came up.

## X thread

1/7 We asked AI which action camera to buy, 118 times. Three brands own the answer, and they don't get equal treatment. Here's the breakdown 🧵

2/7 Who gets named: DJI in every answer. GoPro 98%. Insta360 95%. Akaso 8%. Mentions split almost evenly between the top three.

3/7 The catch is order. DJI is usually named 1st, GoPro 2nd, Insta360 3rd. Being in the answer isn't the prize. Being first is.

4/7 The models disagree too. The OpenAI model recommended Insta360 in 97% of answers. DeepSeek: 70%. Same questions, same day.

5/7 We asked AI to describe Insta360 25 times. “Capture everything, frame later”: 20 of 25. Its newest direct-to-share story: 0 of 25. AI learns your story slowly and forgets it slower.

6/7 Then we tested a Leica partnership message on role-played buyers. “Better image quality with Leica” lifted interest on both models. A bare “Leica certified” line didn't. Sell the outcome, not the name.
