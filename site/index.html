04 / The evidence layer

What grounds every study.

Step 01 of every study, expanded. The panel reacts to what AI actually says about you, not what a workshop imagined. Four measured questions build the stimulus.

01

Presence

Are you in the set of brands AI considers at all? We measure how often you appear, across engines and phrasings.

02

Position

When AI is asked to compare, do you win? We measure head to head recommendation rates against each named competitor.

03

Perception

How does AI describe you? We extract the attributes and sentiment attached to your brand, split by whether the prompt planted them.

04

Proof

Is the story true, and sourced? We check every claim about you against your fact sheet and log what AI gets wrong.

This measured evidence is the stimulus every study is conditioned on.

Every study output carries this stamp. Not a caveat. The feature.

Every Resonance figure ships in the Glass Box: labeled measured or simulated, gated by sample size, wrapped in its confidence interval where the math supports one, and traceable to the stored raw response behind it.

Panels are conditioned on age and income, the two axes validated in independent published research. Reactions are elicited in free text, never gameable numeric ratings, and scored against benchmarked anchor statements. SSR was published and validated by independent researchers: 90% of human test-retest reliability across 9,300 human responses and 57 surveys (arXiv:2510.08338). That validation belongs to the method, not to us. Our work is productizing it: we run it on framings measured from the AI channel, and we label everything we cannot yet prove. Benchmarked, not black-boxed. Uncalibrated scales ship labeled as such.
