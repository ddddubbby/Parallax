AI knows SK Jewellery. What would make it recommend the brand?

SK Jewellery appeared in 97.1% of 139 sampled AI answers. We examined how the models described the brand, then tested alternative messages through AI recommendations and simulated buyer responses.

Windtunnel Research20 Sept 202615 min read + evidence
MeasuredSimulatedAPI study · ungrounded

A brand can appear frequently in AI answers and still face an unanswered marketing question: which reasons does AI give shoppers to choose it? For SK Jewellery, we examined both the brand’s presence and the framing around it, then tested alternative messages under controlled conditions.

What the study tells us

Measured

SK appeared frequently in the answers we sampled.

For a marketing team, the first decision is whether to invest in basic recognition or in the reasons a brand gets chosen. SK appeared in 135 of 139 eligible unbranded answers: 97.1%, with a 95% Wilson interval of 92.8–98.9%. These are questions that did not name the brand.

The two model routes were different: SK appeared in 100.0% of DeepSeek answers (n=70) and 94.2% of OpenAI answers (n=69). The combined rate describes this prompt mix and these model versions; it is not a market-wide visibility score.

SK was organically recommended in 100 of 139 eligible answers (71.9%; 95% Wilson interval 64.0–78.7%). A mention and a recommendation answer different questions. Neither is the controlled top-five outcome used later in this article.

Interpretation: broad recognition is already present in this sample. The next useful question is what information makes SK relevant to a particular buying decision. The zero counts for other tracked names show absence in this sample, not absence from AI knowledge.

SimulatedAI recommendation

We tested whether a different message changed recommendations.

We supplied one recorded AI description as the Current message and an alternative as the New message, separately, to the same shopping situations. The shopping assistant was instructed to use the supplied material only when relevant and return five ranked recommendations. We measured whether SK appeared, and whether it ranked first.

Method

How we tested it—and what remains unresolved.

Audit: 45 prompt cells × five repetitions × two API models produced 450 stored answers on 19 Sep 2026. Six answers had invalid extractions and were excluded from metric populations. The unbranded visibility population contains 139 valid answers from 140 planned unbranded outputs. The separate description lane contains 50 stored answers from five brand-named prompts across both models.

Models: DeepSeek deepseek-flash and OpenAI gpt-5.5-2026-04-23 for the audit; OpenAI gpt-5.5-2026-04-23 for both message tests. All routes were ungrounded: no live web retrieval was performed. These API outputs are not a reproduction of consumer chat interfaces.

Recommendation experiment: fourteen fixed scenarios, five repetitions per message, 140 valid outputs in total. The parser required five distinct ranked brands. The primary comparison averages scenario-level top-five differences; its 95% interval resamples scenario clusters. The counts in each directional row remain dependent repeated outputs from one scenario.

Buyer-response experiment: six specified profiles, five reactions per message, 60 valid outputs. SSR scores free-text reactions by embedding similarity to versioned anchor statements, producing five-point distributions and their means. The article reports point-estimate differences only. These are simulated reactions, not surveyed customers.

Audit rates use the configured tracked-brand extraction set. Neutral brand descriptions never enter mention-rate denominators. Intervals quantify sampling uncertainty within this design; they do not establish representativeness across models, all shopping questions or people. Repeated answers to the same prompts may be correlated.

The supplied sourcing statements are experimental material. This report has not independently certified SK’s sourcing practices. The two baselines are single selected model answers, not summaries of all existing AI knowledge. Any unsupported facts inside them remain attributed model output.

A length-matched control has not run. Added detail and message length remain alternative explanations for any observed movement. A small positive buyer-response mean and a recommendation interval spanning zero do not together establish a business benefit.
